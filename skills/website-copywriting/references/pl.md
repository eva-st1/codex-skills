# Polish website copy

Use for Polish wording and localization. The brand's established voice and requested formality take precedence over these editorial defaults.

## Voice and idiom

Write natural Polish rather than preserving English sentence order. Prefer concrete actions, services, places, and next steps over abstract marketing nouns. Keep a local service's first-person commitment when that is the source voice.

Useful CTA examples include `Zadzwoń`, `Umów termin`, `Wyślij zapytanie`, `Sprawdź zakres`, and `Poproś o wycenę`. Choose only an action the destination actually supports. Headings such as `Zakres prac`, `Usługi`, and `Kontakt` often work without extra framing; use questions when they are natural to the content.

Review calques and empty sales language in context. Phrases such as `dedykowane rozwiązania`, `odkryj nasze usługi`, and `bezszwowa obsługa` often obscure the actual offer. Replace unsupported `kompleksowe`, `profesjonalne`, and `indywidualne podejście` with specific scope or proof. Do not ban a legitimate domain term merely because it also appears in weak marketing.

Prefer `skontaktuj się` to formal nominalizations. Keep labels in one language unless a deliberate, audience-appropriate term is established. Preserve official names and qualifications. Use natural inflection for places and service areas.

## Address and precision

Keep `Państwo` and informal second-person forms consistent within a surface. Direct imperatives can suit local services without creating artificial familiarity. Preserve the requested capitalization convention.

Make tax, price, and invoice labels precise from supplied facts; do not infer VAT status from an abbreviation. Use confirmed dates for experience wording and preserve the source's precision.

## Rendering

Check diacritics, dates, currency, labels, and line wrapping. Where supported, bind one-letter function words (`a`, `i`, `o`, `u`, `w`, `z`) to the following word in presentation. Inspect the result, especially headings and buttons, without imposing hard spaces on canonical data.
