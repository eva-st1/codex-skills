# Norwegian Bokmål website copy

Use Bokmål for a requested Bokmål or unspecified Norwegian variant when context supports it. Preserve an established Nynorsk voice or an explicit language choice; do not silently convert it.

Write natural Norwegian rather than substituting Norwegian words into English syntax. Keep service and portfolio copy concrete: field, place, work type, and next action. Prefer short active sentences to noun-heavy consultant language.

Useful CTAs include `Send en forespørsel`, `Ta kontakt`, `Se utvalgt arbeid`, `Avtal en prat`, and `Send prosjektinformasjon`. Choose a route that exists and fits the business. Use `søk` for a real application, not as a literal translation of an unrelated English action.

Review inflated phrases such as `oppdag våre tjenester` and `skreddersydde løsninger` for substance. Use direct wording where it is more informative. Preserve verified English production titles, qualifications, and specialist terms when translation would change meaning.

Use a neutral professional tone unless the brand supplies another voice. `Du` can fit a direct service page; avoid arbitrary switches to institutional formality. For portfolios, let the work and the person's contribution carry the claim. Do not invent availability, awards, or collaboration traits.

Check `æ`, `ø`, `å`, compound words, place phrasing, currency, and date conventions. Portfolio captions should describe the actual medium, role, or production rather than imply an unsupported outcome.
