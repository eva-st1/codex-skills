# Research and source policy

## Evidence and editorial judgment

Distinguish platform documentation, external research, the author's experience, opinion, and editorial choices. AIDA, title choices, article structures, and voice cards are writing heuristics; they do not establish an algorithmic advantage or guaranteed reach.

Use current LinkedIn primary documentation for format support, eligibility, limits, and newsletter behavior. Read studies before using their results, and preserve population and causal limits. General practitioner advice may inspire an experiment but cannot establish how LinkedIn ranks content.

Do not prescribe a universal word count, posting time, hashtag quota, or publishing cadence. A newsletter is an ongoing editorial choice, not the default outcome of writing one article.

## Primary sources

Checked 2026-10-20:

- [LinkedIn Help: posting updates versus publishing articles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a522483/differences-between-posting-updates-and-publishing). Distinguishes short feed updates from longer articles. Recheck limits rather than embedding them as permanent rules.
- [LinkedIn Marketing Solutions: impactful thought leadership](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/content-marketing/creating-a-thought-leadership-marketing-plan). Guidance on audience relevance, useful expertise, and editorial planning; marketing guidance is not causal proof of performance.

The inherited [LinkedIn Sharing Guide](https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/help/linkedin/en-us/LinkedIn-Sharing-Guide.pdf) redirected to a PDF inaccessible to the research tool on 2026-10-20. No length or performance claim in this skill relies on that document.

## Refresh

For current platform advice or a timely angle, reopen the relevant primary sources and record retrieval date, publication/event date when available, and the supported claim. A historical access date is not current verification. If access fails, disclose the gap and continue with supplied evidence and editorial work that does not depend on it.
