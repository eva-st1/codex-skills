# Create CV

Make the strongest relevant career evidence easy for a person to understand and a document parser to extract. Tailoring means selection and clarification, not fabrication.

## Scope and inputs

Establish the target vacancy or role family, market/language, available career evidence, candidate preferences, employer instructions, and requested output. Use supplied CVs, profile exports, career notes, work samples, and the candidate's answers. A particular profile application, database, account, CLI, renderer, or fact-ID scheme is not required.

For a general CV without a vacancy, work against the stated role family and label the targeting assumption. For a revision, inspect the supplied version and active feedback before changing it. Do not demand a complete company research packet to fix wording or layout.

## Establish the evidence boundary

Use candidate-supplied records and explicit accounts as source evidence. Keep a lightweight claim-to-source map for material titles, dates, responsibilities, results, metrics, skills, credentials, and language levels. A file/section, note, or confirmed answer is sufficient provenance; use existing identifiers if the user's system provides them.

Resolve consequential contradictions and ambiguous ownership. Ask a few focused questions only when the answer could materially improve positioning or factual accuracy. Do not require separate verification ceremonies for facts already clearly supplied or confirmed. Exclude or visibly flag unresolved claims in a draft; do not pass them off as finalized evidence.

Never turn participation into ownership, responsibility into a measured outcome, exposure into proficiency, a team result into an individual result, or an approximate figure into an exact one. Keep public research about an employer separate from evidence about the candidate.

## Draft or revise

Map important requirements to evidence and identify gaps. Choose a positioning thesis and order content by relevance and strength. Use the vacancy's terminology only when it accurately describes the candidate's experience. Preserve official titles; add a functional clarification where needed without rewriting seniority.

Default to a readable reverse-chronological professional CV, adapted to the role and employer's format. Keep skills contextual and language levels accurate. Non-numeric outputs can be strong evidence; never invent metrics to fill a bullet formula.

Honor explicit candidate preferences and prior corrections. Compare revisions with the latest accepted version when supplied. Explain material removals or compression tradeoffs; do not silently delete a distinct role or requested element merely because a template overflows. Keep revision notes proportional to the task rather than imposing a database schema or universal scorecard.

Consider proof links when they strengthen the application. The CV must still stand alone. For AI-tool claims, show actual work, human decisions, verification, and supported outcomes; a tool name alone does not establish expertise.

## Produce and verify

Return text for a text request. For files, use the user's requested format and available document tools; follow the applicable repository or document workflow. PDF, DOCX, or another accepted format may be sufficient—do not require an unrelated HTML artifact. Keep candidate content in the user's chosen private destination, outside a public skills repository.

Validate factual integrity, requirement coverage, language, extraction order, and rendered layout as appropriate. Inspect every page of a generated document and compare extracted text with intended content. If rendering or extraction is unavailable, deliver the usable draft/source and state the unverified checks without claiming submission readiness.

Provide the revised CV or artifact links, relevant checks, and material omissions or unresolved questions. Do not promise an interview, a particular ATS score, or AI-screening success. A request to prepare a CV does not authorize submitting an application, publishing career data, or changing a connected profile.
