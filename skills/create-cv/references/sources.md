# Source policy

The workflow adapts a repository-specific professional CV method into portable instructions. Evidence mapping, positioning, compression, proof selection, and review questions are editorial methods; they are not a validated hiring score or a claim of guaranteed results.

## Lookup starting points and limitations

- The former USAJOBS resume-detail URL redirected to the [USAJOBS Help Center](https://usajobshelp.opm.gov/hc/en-us) on 2026-10-20. Find current detailed instructions and read the actual announcement before asserting federal fields or page limits.
- [CareerOneStop formatting guidance](https://www.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx) returned HTTP 403 to the research tool on that date. It was not independently verified for this adaptation.
- [Europass](https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv) is a lookup starting point when that format is requested; no current feature claim here depends on an uninspected page.

## Updating or applying claims

Reopen relevant official sources for changing platform behavior, legal/privacy requirements, application limits, or a request for current guidance. Record retrieval date, publication/update date when available, exact claim, and jurisdiction/product scope. If access fails, disclose the limitation and do not silently substitute an old claim as current fact.

Career-service advice is practice guidance. Vendor documentation describes a particular product. Laws, market custom, and candidate preferences are separate. Primary studies can inform choices only with their population and causal limitations preserved. Practitioner examples may suggest a review question but cannot prove an exact recruiter review time, universal page count, or predicted interview probability.
